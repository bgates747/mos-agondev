## PB4 chip-select read through the ZDS compatibility facade

Extender ExExt physical VBlank work must defer while stock MOS selects SD via
active-low PB4. The initial product build exposed PB_DR as AgonDev's numeric
address rather than a volatile register, so the compiled guard vanished. The
actual linked-eZ80 regression rejected that image before deployment. Add PB_DR
(0x9A) to the same checked I/O facade used for PC/PD and UART; extend the compile
probe to require an actual IN0 at port154. No MOS product behavior lives here.
The product owner retains physical SD scheduling and hardware qualification.
